What VeloAnalyzer does

A field guide for MTB cross-country racing: every tracked start list enriched with official UCI rank and points, and a season of history behind every rider on it.

  • 🏁Start lists— UCI rank & points, color-coded by tier
  • 👤Rider profiles— 12 months of history, chart included
  • ⚔Head-to-head— any two riders, every shared race
  • 📊Field strength— nation, team & ranking breakdown
  • 🏆Results— official finishes once a race is run
  • 🔄Always current— rebuilt daily from official UCI data

How points are counted

A rider's UCI points total follows the individual XCO ranking rules (art. 4.16.008): each class of race — World Cup and Championship results are uncapped, Class 1–3 one-day races count the best five, and stage races count the best three — feeds its own quota rather than one flat "best N" across everything. A rider's profile shows exactly which results count toward the total and which were displaced.

Ties are broken by the UCI rule: the rider with the more recent point-scoring result ranks higher, regardless of the tier that result came from.

Where the data comes from

Start lists are pulled from organizers' own timing platforms (RaceResult, sportsoft.cz and others), matched to UCI rider records by UCI ID where available and by name and birth year otherwise. Rankings and race history come from the UCI's own data feed and are refreshed daily.

Missing a race?

The home page has a form to suggest a start list that isn't tracked yet — a link to the organizer's or timing provider's entry list is all that's needed.
