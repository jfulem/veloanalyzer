Know who is actually racing, before the gun goes.

A start list is just a list of names. VeloAnalyzer matches every rider against the official UCI ranking and their last twelve months of race results, so you can see the strength of a field at a glance — who the favourites are, who is in form, and how any two riders have fared against each other.

What you can do

🏁 Read a start list properly

Every entrant with their UCI rank, points and team, colour-coded by ranking tier so the sharp end of the field stands out immediately.

👤 Rider profiles

Twelve months of official UCI results per rider — every finish, not only point-scoring ones — with a form chart and which results actually count toward their ranking points.

⚔ Head-to-head

Pick any two riders in a field and see every race they have both started, newest first, with finishing positions side by side.

📊 Field strength

Starter counts by nation and team, best and average UCI rank, and how many riders come from the world's top 50, 100 and 200.

🗓 Race comparison

Every tracked race in one table, upcoming and past, so you can compare the depth of one weekend's field against another.

🔄 Kept current

Start lists and UCI rankings refresh automatically every day, and past races are rebuilt from official UCI results once they have been run.
